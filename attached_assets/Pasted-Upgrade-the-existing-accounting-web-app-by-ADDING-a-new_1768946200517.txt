2. User Input (VERY IMPORTANT)
For EACH cash product, the user must ONLY enter:
- Quantity given
- Quantity left (returned stock)

3. Automatic Calculations (Per Product)
- Quantity sold = Quantity given − Quantity left
- Product cash value = Quantity sold × Unit price

4. Cash Products Total
- Sum all product cash values into:
  “Total Cash Products Revenue”

5. Global Inputs (ONCE)
Add two inputs:
- Old Debt Paid (GHS)
- Expenses (GHS)

6. Final Cash Revenue
Final Cash Revenue =
Existing Cash Sales
+ Total Cash Products Revenue
+ Old Debt Paid
− Expenses

7. Reports
Display clearly:
- Total Cash Products Revenue
- Old Debt Paid
- Expenses
- Final Cash Revenue

8. Validation
- Quantity left must not exceed quantity given
- No negative values
- Automatic recalculation on input change
